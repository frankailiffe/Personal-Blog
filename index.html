About Me!

Hi, I'm Frankie, a 2nd Year Undergraduate in Chemistry at Oriel College, University of Oxford.

I aspire to work in research, and am passionate about scientific communication and teaching.

My favourite 1st Year lecture course was, "States of Matter" by Prof. Jonathan Doye. My favourite 1st Year Lab was, "Chemistry of Interhalogens." I particularly enjoy laboratory work, and recently recieved the Shimadzu Prize in Practical Chemistry (26').

Outside chemistry, I play university ice hockey. I am the club's welfare officer invested in connecting with the playerbase and developing a sense of community. Drawing, illustration and writing occupy the rest of my time.
