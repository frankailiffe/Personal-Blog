Hi, I'm Frankie, a second-year MChem Chemistry student at the University of Oxford, Oriel College.

I am interested in chemistry as a bridging science, connecting subjects from biology to physics and making interdisciplinary work possible. We interact with chemistry everyday; soap, detergents, medicines, refrigerants, textiles and more, are all products of years of chemical research, development and understanding.

My current interests include; analytical chemistry, physical chemistry, chemical crystallography, organic synthesis, spectroscopy and the history of chemistry.

Outside chemistry, I play ice hockey for Oxford University's mixed team and Women's Blues. I am the club's welfare officer - I am invested in connecting with the playerbase and developing a sense of community. Drawing, illustration and writing occupy the rest of my time.
